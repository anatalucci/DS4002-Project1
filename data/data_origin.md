The data came from a website by UCSD called "Amazon Reviews'23." The site had over thirty different datasets, grouped by category such as appliances, beauty etc. 
We chose to work with the electronics set which had 43.9 million rows across 1.6 million unique products. We only wanted to look at the reviews for one product, so we cleaned the dataset as 
as shown in the initial_cleaning.ipynb to reduce the size substantially. The csv saved after cleaning in initial_cleaning.ipynb was our base point to begin the work for the project.
